# viewer app

**C4V-13** · web-application · `public/*.js, stage.css`

Zoom, hover, drill and the flow animation. Client side, no build step.

## Where it sits

A container: something that runs or stores, and could be restarted on its own. Drill it to see the components inside. Shown on the **C2 · the containers inside c4-view** level.

## What to know

No build step and no framework: the page ships as it is, and every behaviour degrades to a plain link when scripting is off.
